# Trademark Policy

The names **turborg** and **xshellz**, and the turborg and xshellz logos, are
trademarks of **xshellz**, the organization that stewards and supports the
turborg open-source project. The Apache 2.0 license under which turborg is
distributed grants you broad rights over the source code, but does **not** grant
you rights to use these trademarks.

## What you can do

- Use the name "turborg" to truthfully describe your project as being built on,
  using, or compatible with turborg. Examples that are fine:
  - "powered by turborg"
  - "a turborg connector for Discord"
  - "MyBot uses turborg under the hood"
- Link to the official turborg repository, website, and documentation.
- Use the unmodified turborg logo to link back to the official project.

## What requires permission

- **Forks**: if you maintain a fork that diverges meaningfully from upstream,
  you must rename it. Do not call your fork "turborg" or use a name likely to
  cause confusion (e.g. "turborg-pro", "turborg-plus", "real-turborg").
- **Distributions and packages**: do not publish a package on any registry
  (Docker Hub, Homebrew, Go module proxy, etc.) under the name "turborg" or
  a confusingly similar name.
- **Hosted services**: do not run a public hosted service called "turborg" or
  "turborg cloud". The official hosted offering is **hive.xshellz.com**.
- **Merchandise, swag, or marketing materials** using the turborg or xshellz
  name or logo.

If you want to do any of the above, open an issue or email
**legal@xshellz.com** to request permission.

## Why this exists

The Apache 2.0 license intentionally separates copyright (which we license
liberally) from trademarks (which we do not). This policy is the soft moat
that lets the source stay open while keeping a single canonical, trustworthy
"turborg" that the community can rely on.

This policy is loosely modeled on the trademark policies of [Mozilla][1],
[Apache][2], and [Linux Foundation][3] projects.
